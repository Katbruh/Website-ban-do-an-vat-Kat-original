# Screenshot review: Maika storefront

Reviewed 2026-11-04 from four user-supplied desktop screenshots. They show the storefront at roughly 1534 pixels wide in dark appearance, across several scroll positions; the captured image heights vary.

Follow-up: the app and design system are now light-only; the dark theme mapping is no longer generated. These screenshots are the pre-change baseline; the updated pages have not been re-rendered for visual review.

## Observed

- The brand, hero message, category filters, search controls, menu cards, pickup information, and footer read as one consistent Maika storefront. Orange accents stand out clearly against the charcoal background.
- Several different menu items reuse the same food photos (including snack trays, spring rolls, and tea). Since customers choose food partly by its photo, replace these illustrative repeats with item-specific photos before presenting the menu as real inventory.
- The final visible menu row contains one card aligned to the left. This leaves open space on the right, but the supplied screenshots do not establish whether that is a problem at other widths or with different menu data.

## Scope limits

These images provide a visual read of the storefront in dark appearance only. They do not show the cart, login, admin, phone/tablet layouts, light appearance, interactive states, keyboard behavior, or motion. Their viewport is also different from the review plan's 1280×900 desktop cell, so they do not count as a completed planned cell. The automated review report remains incomplete for those checks.
